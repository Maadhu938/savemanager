Privacy Policy & Cookie Disclosure

Effective Date: September 27, 2026. Your privacy and trust are paramount to SaveManager.

Home / Privacy Policy

1. Introduction & Overview

SaveManager ("we", "our", or "us"), available at https://savemanager.site/, provides browser-based media analysis and manifest extraction tools for creators, researchers, and digital archivists. This Privacy Policy details the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our website, along with our compliance with international data protection regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Google AdSense Partner Policies.

2. Data Minimization & Zero Media Storage

We operate under a strict principle of data minimization:

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register an account, log in, or provide personal identifying information (such as your name or credit card) to use SaveManager.
  • Zero Video Caching: We do not store, copy, index, or archive any videos, reels, photos, or audio files processed through our platform. All data is parsed in real time and delivered directly to your client browser.
  • Local Browser History: The "Media Vault" feature stores download history solely within your device's browser using HTML5 localStorage. This data never touches or leaves our servers, and you can clear it anytime by clicking "Clear All Vault" in the UI.

3. Log Files & Automated Information Collection

Like most standard web servers, SaveManager utilizes log files to monitor operational performance, mitigate DDoS attacks, and maintain service security. The information inside log files includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized in analytical reporting)
  • Browser type, language, and operating system
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP) and network routing metrics
  • Date and time stamps of incoming requests
  • Referring and exit pages to measure traffic flow

This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information and is automatically purged on a rolling 30-day schedule.

4. Cookies, Web Beacons & Third-Party Advertising

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to ensure basic site functionality (such as remembering your Dark or Light theme preference).

Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

We partner with third-party advertising vendors, including Google, to display advertisements on our website. Please note the following critical terms regarding third-party ad serving:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on savemanager.site.
  • Google’s use of the DART cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and other sites across the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you may opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info or the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance.
Cookie Consent: When you access SaveManager, you can manage your cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings. Blocking non-essential analytical or advertising cookies will not impair your ability to extract and download media manifests.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR (EU / EEA Residents)

If you are an individual located within the European Economic Area (EEA), you possess statutory rights under Articles 15 through 22 of the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • The Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data held by us.
  • The Right to Rectification: You may request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • The Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request deletion of any personal data associated with your IP address where no legal retention requirement exists.
  • The Right to Object or Restrict Processing: You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data under specific conditions.
  • The Right to Data Portability: You may request that we transfer collected data to another organization or directly to you in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please email us directly at privacy@savemanager.site. We respond within 30 calendar days.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected about them over the past 12 months.
  • Request deletion of personal information collected from them.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: SaveManager does not sell, rent, or trade any consumer personal information to third parties for monetary compensation.
  • Non-discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

SaveManager does not knowingly solicit or collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has submitted personal details on our site without parental consent, please contact us immediately at privacy@savemanager.site, and we will promptly remove such records from our logs.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological advancements, legal requirements, or changes to our advertising partnerships. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Effective Date" header.
