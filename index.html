Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download an Instagram Reel or video on iPhone or Android? +

Open the Instagram or Facebook app, find the Reel or video you want, tap the Share button, and select Copy Link. Paste the link into the SaveManager search bar above and click Extract. Once processed, tap Save. On Android, it downloads straight to your Gallery; on iPhone, it saves to your Safari Downloads folder, where you can tap the file and select "Save Video" to add it to your Photos app.

Does SaveManager add any watermark to the downloaded videos? +

No. SaveManager does not add any watermark, logo, or stamp to your downloads. You receive the clean, original video as published by the creator.

Can I extract and download audio (MP3) from videos? +

Yes. Whenever you process a video from Instagram, Facebook, or YouTube, SaveManager provides discrete format options including Audio Only (320kbps MP3 / AAC) so you can save sound tracks, podcasts, speeches, or background music.

Where are the downloaded files saved on my device? +

On Windows and Mac, files are placed in your default Downloads directory. On Android, videos appear in your Downloads manager and Gallery. On iPhone and iPad, downloads are saved to the Files app under the "Downloads" folder.

Is SaveManager completely free with no download limits? +

Yes, SaveManager is 100% free with unlimited downloads. There are no hourly caps, daily quotas, subscriptions, or paywalls.

Can I download private Instagram or Facebook reels? +

To adhere to user privacy and platform security guidelines, SaveManager only processes publicly accessible media. Posts, reels, and stories from private accounts cannot be extracted without personal account credentials.

Do I need to install any app or browser extension? +

No software or extension installation is needed. SaveManager is a web utility that works directly inside any web browser on your phone, tablet, or desktop computer (Safari, Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave).

What video quality and resolutions are supported? +

SaveManager extracts the highest available master resolution uploaded by the creator, including 1080p Full HD, 720p HD, and original resolution 4K images for Pinterest pins.
